How long does NYC take to close a 311 request?

Every 311 service request created in 2025, from NYC Open Data (dataset erm2-nwe9). Requests still open when the data was pulled are kept as censored rather than dropped; wait times are Kaplan-Meier estimates.

Share of requests still open, by agency

Kaplan-Meier survival curves. Hours since the request was created, log scale. Parks and TLC requests are the ones that stay open for months.

Dropping open requests understates the wait

Median time to close per agency: the naive median over closed requests only, and the Kaplan-Meier median that counts still-open requests as waiting.

The 20 most common complaint types

Kaplan-Meier median (dot) and 90th percentile (end of bar), log scale.

Heat and hot water complaints through the year

Request volume and median time to close, by month created.

Data quality

Every row is kept and given a verdict; the analysis reads only rows marked ok.

Source: NYC Open Data, 311 Service Requests. Code and method: github.com/Revind4k/nyc311-response-times.
